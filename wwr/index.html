Trip Types

We offer a range of trips for different experience levels, from absolute beginner to expert-supreme. You can join our beginner rafting classes, or book a trip with one of our amazing guides to have the adventure of a lifetime.

  • Half-Day Trips (Beginner and Family Friendly)
  • Day Trips
  • Overnight Trips (Intermediate)
  • Weekend Trips
  • Multi-Day Trips (Experienced)

Accommodations

We offer reasonably priced gear rentals (includig helmets, life-jackets, wet suits, etc.)

Tent camping areas and RV hookups are located near the main office and launching areas. For weekend and longer multi-day trips, camping and/or sleeping accommodations are included with the booking. Meals are also included in multi-day trips by our cook.

Our media specialist will accommpany your trip to take photographs and video of your adventure (digital downloads included in booking price).
