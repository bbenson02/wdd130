Utah

Utah is located in the Western United States of America. Utah is known for its vastly diverse landscapes. From the snow peaked Wasatch Mountains, to the Great Salt Lake and Salt Flats, to the red rock arches and canyons in the south, Utah has something for everyone. Utah is nicknamed "The Beehive State" after the first Latter-Day Saints who worked so hard to survie in the desert of Utah.
